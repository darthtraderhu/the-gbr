## What changed

**Headline:** **It became two separate searches**

**Left — the classic result page**
> **Google's technical bar has risen.** Load speed, layout stability and response time are ranking factors now, not comfort issues. A slow site doesn't lose because it's ugly — it loses because Google ranks it lower.

**Right — the AI answer**
> **And a second route appeared.** More people simply ask an AI. These systems don't read keywords, they read structure. If that structure isn't machine-readable, someone else appears in the answer.

---

## The two pillars

**01 · SEO — Technical foundation**
> What Google measures. Server-side rendering means the crawler sees content immediately — no waiting for a browser to assemble it.

*(Core Web Vitals chips: LCP, INP, CLS active; FID struck through, "retired in 2024".)*

- **Server-side rendering** — the crawler reads content immediately
- **Automatic sitemap and robots.txt** — new pages included without anyone remembering
- **Core Web Vitals** — the three metrics Google actually measures
- **Canonicals and redirects** — the most expensive mistake to skip during a migration

**02 · AEO — AI visibility**
> What language models read. We can't guarantee an AI cites you — nobody can. What we can do is make sure **there's something worth citing**: clean structure, unambiguous data, machine-readable.

- **Schema.org and JSON-LD** — company data, articles, services, FAQs
- **Structured FAQs** — can appear directly in results, before the click
- **Unambiguous company data** — the same everywhere, no contradictions
- **Content that answers questions** — not keyword stuffing

---

## Process

**01 · Assessment**
> Where you stand: speed, indexing, structure, structured data. You get a list of what's missing — whether or not you continue with us.

**02 · Build**
> Fixing the technical foundation. If the current system can't carry it, a rebuild — but phased, so you don't lose traffic on the way.

**03 · Maintenance**
> Search engines change, and so does your site. If you want operations too, we track it.

---

## FAQ

**How long before results show?**
> Technical fixes appear in indexing within weeks. Ranking movement is slower — three to six months for competitive terms. Anyone promising faster either doesn't understand it or isn't being honest.

**Do you guarantee first position?**
> No, and avoid anyone who does. Google decides ranking, not your supplier. What we can guarantee: the technical foundation will be sound, measurable, and verifiable by you.

**Is AEO actually worth it?**
> It brings little traffic today, but it's growing fast — and the structured data it requires also helps in conventional Google results. So it isn't wasted spend even if AI search grows slower than expected.

**Can you improve an existing site, or only build new?**
> Both. The assessment tells you which is better value — sometimes fixing the current system is cheaper and faster.

**How do I know you actually did it?**
> The same way you can check us: sitemap, robots.txt, Rich Results Test, PageSpeed. Public, free tools — you don't have to take our word for it.

# NOTES FOR THE DEVELOPER

## URL structure

Recommended: `/en/...` prefix, Hungarian at root without a prefix. This keeps existing Hungarian URLs unchanged — no redirects, no lost indexing.

## hreflang

Every page needs reciprocal `hreflang` tags: `hu`, `en`, and `x-default` pointing at the Hungarian version.

## What must not be duplicated

**Article slugs.** If an article exists in both languages, the English one needs a distinct slug and a `hreflang` link to its Hungarian pair. Untranslated articles should not appear in the English list at all — better a short honest selection than empty pages.

## Structured data

All schemas need an English variant with `inLanguage: "en"`. The `Organization` schema stays the same entity — only the descriptions change.

## Currency

The budget options are in euros, not forints. Agency clients budget in euros; converting would look improvised.

# WHAT'S DELIBERATELY MISSING

**No client logos, no testimonials.** Until someone has agreed in writing to be named, there's nothing to show. An empty logo wall is worse than none; a fabricated one is worse still.

**No hourly rate.** Publishing one anchors every negotiation and gets undercut immediately. The advantage is stated as fact — "less than an internal hire" — without a number. Give the number in the conversation.

**No "20 years of experience" as a headline claim.** It carries weight in Hungary; abroad it reads as filler. The engineering evidence does that work instead.

**No claimed results.** No percentages, no conversion lifts. Same rule as the Hungarian site: we state what we deliver, not what it will produce.
